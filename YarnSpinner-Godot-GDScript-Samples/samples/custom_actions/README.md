# Custom Actions

Demonstrates custom Commands and Functions, including a Function that waits for the player to pick an item, and a Command the player can skip.

Talk to Capsley for an explanation. Collect fruit from the three piles, then bring Hungry Henry the one he asks for.

- Walk with WASD or the arrow keys, and press E to interact.
- When Henry walks off to eat, press Space or Enter to skip his walk.
- Press Esc while picking an item to stop the conversation.
